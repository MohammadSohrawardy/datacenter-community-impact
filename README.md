# U.S. Data Center Community Impact & Readiness Platform

Local governments need clear facts before approving AI data centers. This tool helps answer the hard questions. It models the real economic and resource impacts of new infrastructure.

**Version 1.0 coming soon.**

## Who This Is For
I built this for U.S. cities, counties, Tribal governments, utilities, and policymakers. 

## What It Calculates
This platform takes project assumptions and estimates the following community impacts:
* **Fiscal:** Local tax benefits, public incentives, and net public costs.
* **Power:** Electricity demand and grid load context.
* **Water:** Water withdrawal, consumption, and cooling needs.
* **Jobs:** Separating temporary construction job-years from permanent operating jobs.
* **Infrastructure:** How costs are split between developers, ratepayers, and taxpayers.

## Data Sources
The math relies entirely on verifiable, public data. Core baselines come from authoritative sources like the U.S. Energy Information Administration (EIA), U.S. Geological Survey (USGS), Census Bureau, and Bureau of Economic Analysis (BEA).
